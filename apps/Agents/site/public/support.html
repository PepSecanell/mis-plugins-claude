Support

Agent Teams for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Need help? Email keoly.app@gmail.com. I usually reply within two days.

Getting started

  • Open Settings (the gear) and pick an AI provider: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek or Groq.
  • Paste your API key from that provider's website and tap Save key. The app checks the key and loads the models it can use.
  • Open the chat with Chief, your main agent. Tell it what you want help with, and ask it to build you a team of agents.

Common questions

"Your API key was rejected." Copy the key again from the provider's website, without spaces. Some providers need billing set up before a key works.

"Rate limited" or "out of credit". Your provider account has hit a limit or has no balance. Check your usage on the provider's website.

An agent says it needs a key. Each agent runs on one provider. Open the agent (tap its name in the chat, then the info button) and switch it to a provider you've added a key for.

How much does it cost? The app is free. You pay your AI provider directly, usually a fraction of a cent to a few cents per message depending on the model.

Copy, reply or retry a message. Long-press a message on iPhone or iPad, or right-click it on Mac.

My chats aren't syncing. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on each device and that iCloud Drive is on. Sync can take a minute.

Delete everything. See the privacy policy for how to remove keys, chats and iCloud data.
