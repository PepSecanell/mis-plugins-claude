Privacy Policy

Agent Teams for iPhone, iPad and Mac · Effective 3 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. I (the developer) never receive your chats or anything else you put in the app.
  • The app works with your own API key from an AI provider you choose. Your messages go straight from your device to that provider.
  • Your agents, chats and "About me" facts are stored on your devices and synced between them through your own iCloud account, if iCloud is on.

Who is responsible

Agent Teams is made by Pep Secanell, an independent developer based in Spain. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), I am the data controller for anything this policy covers. You can reach me at keoly.app@gmail.com.

What I collect

Nothing. Agent Teams has no sign-in, no user accounts of mine, no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting services and no third-party tracking code. There is no server of mine involved in using the app.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores your agents (their names, instructions and settings), your chats, and the facts saved in "About me". This data lives:

  • On your device, in the app's own storage.
  • In your private iCloud database, if you are signed in to iCloud, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac stay in sync. This uses Apple's CloudKit. It is stored in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and I cannot access it.

Your API keys

To get answers, you add an API key from one or more AI providers. Each key is saved in your device's Keychain, Apple's secure storage for passwords, and synced to your other devices through iCloud Keychain if you use it. A key is only ever sent to the provider it belongs to, as part of your requests. It is never sent to me.

What is sent to AI providers

Before the app sends anything to a provider, it asks for your permission for that provider. After that, each time an agent replies, the app sends the following directly from your device to that agent's provider, over an encrypted connection:

  • your message and the recent messages in that chat,
  • the agent's instructions and the names and specialties of your other agents,
  • the facts saved in "About me",
  • for agents with web search turned on, the request to search the web (handled by Anthropic).

The supported providers are Anthropic, OpenAI, Google (Gemini), xAI, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek and Groq. You decide which ones to use by adding their keys. Nothing is sent to a provider you haven't added a key for.

The provider's rules apply

Your AI provider is not working for me. You have your own account with them, and they handle what you send under their own terms and privacy policy, including how long they keep it and whether they may use it to improve their services. Please read the policy of each provider you use. Some providers' free tiers allow them to use what you send to improve their products. Avoid sharing passwords or other people's personal data in chats.

Any AI costs are billed to you by your provider. The app itself is free.

Health, money and other advice

Agent Teams is a general-purpose AI assistant. Its answers can be wrong and are not medical, legal or financial advice. Talk to a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Children

Agent Teams is not aimed at children. Because it has no accounts and I collect no data, I don't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. AI providers set their own minimum ages; please check their terms.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Since I don't hold any data about you, there is normally nothing for me to hand over or delete. In practice:

  • To remove a key, open Settings in the app, pick the provider and tap Remove key.
  • To delete a chat or an agent, swipe or long-press it in the app. To delete everything, delete the app from all your devices and remove its data in iCloud settings (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage on iPhone).
  • For anything held by an AI provider, contact that provider directly.

If you think your data has been handled unlawfully, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD) or the authority in your EU country. I'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever started collecting data, I would tell you inside the app before it took effect.
