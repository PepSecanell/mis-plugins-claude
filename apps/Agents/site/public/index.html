How it works

It starts with one conversation.

  1. Tell Chief what you need.

    Chief is your main agent. Say what you want help with and it asks a few questions to get it right.

  2. Chief builds your team.

    It proposes specialists and creates them for you, with names, emoji, instructions and models.

  3. They work on it together.

    In a group chat, Chief answers or hands off to the right agent. When agents consult each other, you can open the panel and read it.

Questions

How much does it cost?

The app is free, with no subscription. You pay your AI provider directly for what you use, usually a fraction of a cent to a few cents per message depending on the model.

What's an API key, and where do I get one?

It's what lets the app use your account with an AI provider. You create one on the provider's website, paste it into Settings, and the app checks it and loads the models it can use.

Some providers need billing set up before a key works.

Which AI providers work?

Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI (Grok), OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek and Groq. Each agent can use a different provider and model.

Can anyone read my chats?

I can't. There's no account and no server of mine involved. Your messages go from your device straight to your provider, and the app asks your permission before sending anything to one.

Your provider handles what you send under its own terms. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it sync between my devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud. Sign in to the same iCloud account on your iPhone, iPad and Mac and your agents and chats stay in sync. Keys sync through iCloud Keychain if you use it.
