🧭 Agent Teams Home

Your own team of AI agents

For iPhone, iPad and Mac

Talk to Chief, your main agent, about what you want help with. Chief builds a team of specialist agents just for you, and they work together in group chats, consult each other and remember what matters about you.

How it works

  • Bring your own AI. Use a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek or Groq. Each agent can run on a different model.
  • Agents that work together. Group chats where the main agent hands off to the right specialist, and private consults you can open and read.
  • Shared memory. Facts about you that every agent knows, which you can see and edit.
  • Private by design. No accounts and no tracking. Chats go straight from your device to your provider and sync through your own iCloud.
