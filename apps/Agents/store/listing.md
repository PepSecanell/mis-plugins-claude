## Description

Agent Teams gives you your own team of AI agents on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Start by talking to Chief, your main agent. Tell it what you want help with, like your health, your studies, your business or a side project. Chief asks a few questions, proposes a team of specialists, and creates them for you. Each agent has its own instructions, personality and model, and you can change any of it by asking Chief or by editing the agent yourself.

YOUR AGENTS WORK TOGETHER
• Group chats where the main agent answers or hands off to the right specialist
• @mention one agent, or @everyone to hear from the whole team
• Agents privately consult each other, and you can open each consult to see who asked what

BRING YOUR OWN AI
• Works with your own API key from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI (Grok), OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek or Groq
• Each agent can run on a different provider and model
• You pay your provider directly. The app is free, with no subscription

THEY REMEMBER YOU
• Shared "About me" memory that every agent can read
• See and edit every fact in Settings

A REAL CHAT APP
• Copy, reply to, share and retry messages
• Web search for agents on Claude models
• Your agents and chats sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac through your iCloud

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your messages go straight from your device to the AI provider you chose, never through the developer's servers
• The app asks for your permission before sending anything to a provider

Answers come from AI and can be wrong. Agent Teams is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.
